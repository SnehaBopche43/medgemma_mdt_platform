
TREATMENT RECOMMENDATION:

For this 52-year-old woman with Stage IIB (T2N1M0) ER+/PR+/HER2- invasive ductal carcinoma:

1. NEOADJUVANT CHEMOTHERAPY:
   - AC-T regimen (Doxorubicin/Cyclophosphamide followed by Paclitaxel)
   - 4 cycles AC followed by 12 weeks Paclitaxel
   
2. SURGERY:
   - Breast-conserving surgery or mastectomy based on tumor response
   - Sentinel lymph node biopsy or axillary dissection
   
3. ADJUVANT HORMONAL THERAPY:
   - Tamoxifen or Aromatase Inhibitor for 5-10 years
   - Consider CDK4/6 inhibitor (Abemaciclib) given high-risk features
   
4. RADIATION THERAPY:
   - Post-surgical radiation to breast/chest wall and regional nodes
